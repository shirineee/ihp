About This Conversation

Mental Wellness & Digital Well-Being

In a world where teens face unprecedented digital and social pressures, raising grounded and independent thinkers requires intentional strategies and compassionate support. This panel discussion dives deep into mental wellness and digital well-being, offering practical insights for parents, educators, and caregivers committed to nurturing resilient teens who can thrive without burnout.

Join us to gain actionable tools and fresh perspectives on supporting teens' mental and digital wellness, empowering them to become independent thinkers ready to face today's high-pressure world with confidence and balance.

Our Panel

Distinguished Speakers

Tiffany Wycoff Founder, Generation Remix

Tiffany Wycoff empowers teens as digital natives to lead conversations about growing up in the digital age. She transforms family dynamics by replacing screen-time conflicts with genuine partnerships, helping parents reclaim peace of mind while teens leverage their tech experiences into leadership and meaningful connections. We will have a panel of the MA Generation Remix Club.

Genoveva La Placa Executive & Emotional Leadership Coach

A former hedge fund CFO turned Executive and Emotional Leadership Coach, Genoveva blends neuroscience, conscious parenting, and leadership strategy. She guides high-achieving families to raise resilient, self-led teens who flourish in competitive environments without sacrificing well-being.

Arin Bass Psychotherapist, LMFT

A compassionate psychotherapist committed to evidence-based care, Arin creates a safe space for clients to explore their inner worlds. Her holistic approach integrates mind, body, and soul therapies to address the root causes of struggles and foster lasting mental wellness.

Shirin Aryanpour Moderator · Founder, Illuminate Her Path

The discussion will be moderated by Shirin Aryanpour, founder of Illuminate Her Path, an impact-driven platform dedicated to spotlighting women doers, uplifting community, and driving social equity. Shirin's leadership and community-building expertise ensure a dynamic and impactful dialogue.
