The Women Behind the Afternoon

Meet Your Hosts & Makers

Ashley Berman Host & Atelier · Ashley Morgan Designs

From her private atelier in Ross, California, designer and trained goldsmith Ashley Berman creates one-of-a-kind fine jewelry built on a simple belief: jewelry should tell a story. Trained at San Francisco's Revere Academy of Jewelry Arts, Ashley has spent more than 20 years working closely with clients on bespoke pieces, and she is especially known for heirloom redesign, turning a grandmother's diamond or a box of inherited gemstones into modern pieces made to be worn now and passed on again.

Her atelier is warm, intimate and collaborative, home to her custom work, singular one-of-a-kind designs, and a curated collection of everyday fine jewelry from trusted artisan partners.

Evelyn Shooshani Featured Designer · Le Noué

Evelyn Shooshani is the founder of Le Noué, a Beverly Hills–based house built on a simple belief: the most beautiful scarves were never meant to stay folded. Each Le Noué bag begins with a single vintage or vintage-inspired piece, found and considered, then paired with a handle made to match. There is no production run, no reorder. Every bag is one of one. Evelyn launched Le Noué at 53, turning a lifelong eye for beautiful textiles into a house entirely her own.

Louisa Sushansky, APRN Featured Speaker · 4:30 PM · Selfishly Aesthetics

Louisa Sushansky, APRN, is a Bay Area–born and raised medical provider with 10 years of clinical experience and a passion for advancing the intersection of aesthetics, wellness, and regenerative medicine. As the founder and clinical leader of a high-volume aesthetics and wellness practice in San Francisco, Louisa is recognized for her expertise in regenerative medicine, wound care, hormone replacement therapy (HRT), peptides, and advanced aesthetic treatments.

Committed to staying at the forefront of her field, Louisa pursues extensive, ongoing education and hands-on training with some of the most respected providers and leaders in the aesthetics and wellness industry. A lifelong Bay Area local, she has built her practice around the belief that self-care is health care, and that true wellness begins from within.

Shirin Aryanpour Co-Host · Founder, Illuminate Her Path

Shirin Aryanpour is the founder of Illuminate Her Path, an impact-driven women's wellness and community platform. A professional background in healthcare shaped her broader view of healing, and today she brings women together through intentional gatherings centered on connection, healing, empowerment and conscious living. A connector, community builder and space holder at heart, Shirin creates rooms where women can slow down, be present and support one another.
